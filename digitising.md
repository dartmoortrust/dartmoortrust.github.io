# Standard Operating Procedures

The Standing Operating Procedures (SOP) set out below are to be used for the scanning and documentation of original documents and images including prints, slides, glass plates, maps, books etc.

The Dartmoor Trust’s aim for its Archive is to preserve in digital form as much of Dartmoor and its environs’ heritage as possible. This is achieved by digitising images, audio and video using best practice archiving techniques. The files are kept in their purest form and stored in multiple locations to ensure that they are safe and available for future generations. A smaller ‘web friendly’ version is then made available, together with associated metadata, on the trust’s website at www.dartmoortrust.org.

## SCANNING REQUIREMENTS

The Trust has always tried to align as closely as possible with the [National Archive Standards](https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/information-management/digitisation-at-the-national-archives.pdf) for scanning and preservation. These are updated periodically as technology and storage improves. The Trust’s current criteria are:

### Ordinary documents

300 PPI (Pixels Per Inch) is the default resolution. 24-bit sRGB colour space.

### Photographic materials

600 PPI for standard photographs. 24-bit sRGB colour space.  
4000 PPI for photographic transparencies. 24-bit sRGB colour space. 3200dpi is acceptable.

### Microform

Resolution should be equivalent to 300 PPI at original document size. 24-bit sRGB color space.  
If not feasible, the maximum possible resolution must be agreed upon. 24-bit sRGB color space.

## Colour Space

24-bit colour or 8-bit grayscale depending on source.

## Storage

Masters are stored as TIFFs. National Archives Standards seems to have changed to using JP2. In the meantime we continue with TIFFs. They can be changed later if there are advantages.

## SURROGATE IMAGES

The Trust is not strict about providing copies as they are often generated upon request and versions are provided that work best for being online over a set standard.

## CROPPING

When scanning you are likely to be asked to crop. It is important to crop manually allowing for a small amount of white space around the item to show that all the image is available. The software may want to automatically crop but this tends to it cutting off the edge of the image.

## IDENTIFICATION AND NUMBERING

Within a single collection or batch of photos, number the images consecutively (1, 2,3 etc) prefaced by the initials of the photographer or donor. Take care not to use letters that have previously been used.

## FRONT AND BACK CAPTIONS

Both front and, if it contains information, the back of the provided material are to be scanned. The 2 scans are to be recorded in the spread sheet as ID number.F and in the next row ID number.B together with the relevant text.

# DOCUMENTATION OF SCANNED MATERIAL

The intention is to record on a provided spreadsheet, any information provided on the original material in a format that enables the webmaster to transfer the metadata into the new Record. Consistency is vital. Where possible, VV will complete the Title from information available from the original and the image.

## Title

The title of the image is used to provide a short description of the record and is shown superimposed on a Record’s thumbnails. Up to 40 characters in sentence case including spaces may be used. Although sentences may be used, it is the keywords that searchers might use that are vital, especially until the full metadata has been added. This will often be the only available searchable metadata until the other fields have been completed, so should be completed wherever possible.

## Detail

This is a significant part of the metadata supporting the Record, the activity is included in Detail. This is searchable using search engines. Ideally written in prose, it should contain as many descriptive words as possible to assist Archive users to search for relevant records. If this is provided to VV, it is to be recorded in the Detail column. If nothing has been provided, the Detail will need to be added to the Record at a later date by Trustees, volunteers and the public.

## Location of Subject of Record

Where available on the original, VV will enter the grid reference on the spreadsheet using the format SX 311 575.

## Direction of Photographer’s view

If provided, the angle of the photographer’s view is to be recorded within the Detail using the 8 cardinal points \< N, E. S or W or NE, SE. SW or NW.

## Adding Metadata to the Records

JD is to enter into the Archive’s Records the information provided by the Trust. And pass the spreadsheet to the Trustee responsible for the Collection. The Trustee is then to verify and, where necessary, elaborate, on the Record’s metadata.

## PROBLEMS?

Contact the Trustee responsible for the digitisation of the collection being dealt with.

If you consider the material provided to be of insufficient quality, put it to one side and contact the Trustee responsible for the collection.
